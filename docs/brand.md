# LocalHub Brand Guidelines

## Identity
LocalHub is the neighborhood compass. We connect locals to authentic, quality community spots. Our tone is warm, confident, and neighborhood-friendly.

## Logo
- **Mark**: A geometric location pin featuring an internal "L" structural path connected by dots, representing nodes of a community.
- **Wordmark**: `DM Sans`, 700 weight, tight letter spacing (`-1px`).
- **Variants**:
  - `logo-full-dark.svg`: Use on light backgrounds (Text color: `#1A1A1A`)
  - `logo-full-light.svg`: Use on dark backgrounds (Text color: `#FAF9F7`)

## Color Palette

### Primary
- **Burnt Orange**: `#E85D2A` - Our core brand color. Used for primary actions, badges, and brand highlights.
- **Hover Orange**: `#D14E1F`
- **Light Orange (Surface)**: `#FFF0EB`

### Accent
- **Forest Green**: `#2D7A4F` - Used for verification badges, success states, and secondary emphasis.
- **Hover Green**: `#236B42`
- **Light Green (Surface)**: `#E8F5EE`

### Neutrals (Light Mode)
- **Background**: `#FAF9F7` - Warm off-white to prevent stark contrast strain.
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - True white for cards and modals.
- **Border**: `#E5E2DC`
- **Text Primary**: `#1A1A1A`
- **Text Secondary**: `#5C5C5C`
- **Text Muted**: `#8A8A8A`

### Neutrals (Dark Mode)
- **Background**: `#121212`
- **Surface**: `#1E1E1E`
- **Border**: `#333333`
- **Text Primary**: `#EAEAEA`
- **Text Secondary**: `#B0B0B0`
- **Text Muted**: `#707070`

## Typography
- **Display / Headings**: `DM Sans` (Google Fonts). Confident, modern geometric sans-serif.
- **Body**: `Inter` (Google Fonts). Highly legible, clean interface sans-serif.

## Imagery
- Authentic, real photos of local businesses.
- **No AI-generated or slop imagery.**
- If no photo is available, use curated category pattern SVGs or high-quality Unsplash fallbacks with proper attribution.
- Empty states should be designed beautifully using Lucide icons. (No emojis as icons).
